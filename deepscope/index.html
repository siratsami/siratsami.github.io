What is Deep Scope?

Deep Scope is an advanced recon platform that passively monitors your target’s infrastructure in real time. From subdomains to DNS records and origin IPs, it provides accurate and actionable visibility — without generating noise. Ideal for offensive security professionals and analysts.

Core Capabilities

Real-Time Subdomain Discovery

New subdomains are detected instantly and added to your monitored list.

Passive DNS Record Tracking

View live changes in A, CNAME, MX, NS, and TXT records for your targets.

Origin IP Discovery

Detect backend servers by filtering out CDN/WAF IPs automatically.

Port Scanning

Quiet scans on origin IPs to find open ports and vulnerable services.

Cloud & Archive Intelligence

Leverages cloud leaks, open repositories, and archives for hidden data.

Alerting & Automation

Be notified the moment something changes. Webhooks and email supported.
